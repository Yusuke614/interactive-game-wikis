1. Basics, Controls & 3DS Version Differences
3DS Remaster Overhauls

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D remasters the timeless Nintendo 64 classic for the Nintendo 3DS at a silky 30 frames per second with stereoscopic 3D, refined textures, updated character models, and a host of quality-of-life gameplay enhancements:

Touchscreen Inventory & Gear QoL Upgrade

The 3DS bottom screen provides instant access to your item inventory, equipment, map, and ocarina songs. You can equip the Iron Boots and Hover Boots as toggleable button items on the touchscreen without ever pausing the game—radically streamlining the Water Temple!

Gyroscope First-Person Aiming Precision Control

When using the Fairy Slingshot, Fairy Bow, Boomerang, or Hookshot/Longshot, you can aim with pinpoint precision by tilting the Nintendo 3DS console using internal gyroscopes (or using the Circle Pad if preferred).

Sheikah Stones & Hint Visions New 3DS Feature

Two glowing green Sheikah Stones are located in Hyrule—one right outside Link's treehouse in Kokiri Forest, and one inside the Temple of Time. Crawling inside lets players view short "Vision" movie clips revealing puzzle hints and hidden Heart Pieces if they are stuck.

The Shard of Agony Secret Detector

Replaces the N64 rumble pak "Stone of Agony". Obtained after collecting 20 Gold Skulltula tokens, this crystalline shard appears in the top-left screen corner and beeps with increasing visual frequency when Link is near hidden grottoes or subterranean holes.

Boss Challenge & Gauntlet Mode Combat Mode

After clearing the Forest Temple as Adult Link, returning to Link's treehouse bed in Kokiri Forest allows you to choose between taking a nap to restore hearts or entering Boss Challenge Mode to battle previously defeated bosses with timed records or run the ultimate 8-boss Gauntlet!

Master Quest (Mirror World) Full Extra Campaign

Unlocked upon beating the main campaign. The entire world of Hyrule is mirrored east-to-west, enemies deal double damage, and all dungeons feature completely redesigned, challenging puzzles and altered item layouts.

Essential Beginner Milestones

Find the Kokiri Sword: Crawl through the hole in the Forest Training Center, dodge the giant rolling boulder, and open the treasure chest.
Buy the Deku Shield (40 Rupees): Hop across the water platforms near the shop and explore Kokiri homes to collect 40 Rupees, then purchase the shield from the Kokiri Shop.
Win the First Empty Bottle: Visit Lon Lon Ranch and win Talon's Super Cucco-catching minigame (catch 3 specific chickens within 30 seconds) to earn Lon Lon Milk in an Empty Bottle.
Acquire the Free Hylian Shield: Go to the Kakariko Graveyard at night, pull the grave with flowers in front of it during the first row, and drop into the crypt to find a free Hylian Shield in a chest.

4. Master Quest Walkthrough & Dungeon Differences
Mirrored World • Double Damage

Unlocked immediately upon completing the standard campaign, Master Quest in Ocarina of Time 3D features a mirrored map layout (everything flipped horizontally), altered enemy placement, double incoming damage, and radically rearranged dungeon puzzles:

Deku Tree (Master Quest) Dungeon 1

Key Differences: Floor webs are guarded by Gohma Larvae clusters. The waterlogged basement requires pushing brown stepping blocks and lighting torches under strict time limits. The scrub order is changed to Left (2), Right (3), Middle (1).

Dodongo's Cavern (Master Quest) Dungeon 2

Key Differences: Giant crates must be pulled across lava rooms to trigger submerged switches. Baby Dodongos spawn in high concentrations. You must detonate rows of Bomb Flowers in chain reactions to lower high stone staircases.

Jabu-Jabu's Belly (Master Quest) Dungeon 3

Key Differences: Features iconic cow switches embedded inside the flesh walls of Jabu-Jabu that must be shot with the Slingshot to open doors. Like-Likes drop from ceilings in tight corridors.

Forest Temple (Master Quest) Dungeon 4

Key Differences: The courtyard puzzles require precision Hookshot traversal to eye switches hidden behind vine walls. The Floormaster and Wallmaster density is doubled, and twisted hallway keys are hidden behind eye switches.

Fire Temple (Master Quest) Dungeon 5

Key Differences: Goron cell cages contain false walls and hidden rusted switches. Hammering totems triggers moving flame walls that require rapid Iron Boots and Hookshot maneuvers.

Water Temple (Master Quest) Dungeon 6

Key Differences: All 3 water control levels are heavily reworked. Bomb walls are hidden behind underwater geysers. Pushing central tower blocks requires floating with Iron Boots and timing Longshot targets on moving spikes.

Shadow Temple (Master Quest) Dungeon 7

Key Differences: Requires extensive use of Din's Fire to burn wooden spikes and ignite torches across bottomless pits. Beamos and ReDead rooms contain invisible guillotines and spinning scythes.

Spirit Temple (Master Quest) Dungeon 8

Key Differences: The child section requires maneuvering silver rupees across narrow moving spiked rollers. The adult section features multi-room sun-mirror chains reflecting light across three distinct floors.

8. Equipment, Capacity Upgrades & Great Fairy Fountains
Swords, Shields & Magic

Equipping specialized tunics, heavy boots, durable shields, and receiving divine blessings from the Great Fairies grants Link elemental resistance, magic spells, and enhanced defensive resilience:

Swords & Blades Offense

• Kokiri Sword: Young Link's standard blade found in the Forest Training Center.
• Master Sword: The legendary Blade of Evil's Bane drawn from the Temple of Time.
• Giant's Knife (200 Rupees): Two-handed blade bought in Goron City; breaks after 8 strikes!
• Biggoron's Sword: Unbreakable legendary two-handed sword dealing double Master Sword damage!

Shields Defense

• Deku Shield: Wooden shield; burns to ash when touching fire.
• Hylian Shield: Heavy steel shield that deflects physical attacks and fireballs.
• Mirror Shield: Polished mirror surface found in Spirit Temple; absorbs and reflects light and magic beams!

Tunics & Boots Elemental Gear

• Goron Tunic: Crimson tunic granting total heat immunity in volcanoes.
• Zora Tunic: Sapphire tunic allowing Link to breathe underwater indefinitely.
• Iron Boots: Heavy boots used to sink into deep waters and resist strong winds.
• Hover Boots: Golden boots that glide horizontally across midair chasms.

Great Fairy Spells Magic

• Din's Fire: Massive expanding dome of flame that burns all surroundings.
• Farore's Wind: Creates warp points inside dungeons to teleport back instantly.
• Nayru's Love: Protective crystalline barrier rendering Link temporarily invulnerable to damage.

Great Fairy Fountains Locations

Death Mountain Summit (Magic Meter & Spin Attack): Bomb the cracked wall outside the crater summit as child; play Zelda's Lullaby to receive the Magic Meter and enhanced Spin Attack.
Hyrule Castle Courtyard (Din's Fire Spell): Bomb the giant boulder past the castle gate, crawl through the crawlspace, and play Zelda's Lullaby to obtain Din's Fire.
Zora's Fountain Corner (Farore's Wind Spell): Bomb the boulder on the small island in the southeastern corner of Zora's Fountain to obtain Farore's Wind.
Death Mountain Crater (Double Magic Meter): As Adult Link with the Megaton Hammer, smash the red rock totems behind the entrance bridge to double your magic capacity.
Desert Colossus (Nayru's Love Spell): Bomb the cracked wall between the two palm trees outside the Spirit Temple to obtain the shield spell Nayru's Love.
Ganon's Castle Exterior (Double Defense): Lift the massive grey monolith outside Ganon's Castle using the Golden Gauntlets to receive white hearts that halve all incoming damage!

9. Side Quests, Trading Sequences & Empty Bottles
Biggoron & Masks

Mastering Hyrule's multi-step trading sequences grants the ultimate unbreakable two-handed blade, mystical masks, and all four versatile Empty Bottles:

The Biggoron's Sword 11-Step Trading Quest

Done Step & Item Trade Partner & Location Instructions & Timed Speedrun Shortcuts
1. Pocket Egg Cucco Lady (Kakariko) Receive the Pocket Egg from the Cucco Lady. Wait for dawn for it to hatch into the Pocket Cucco.
2. Cojiro Talon & Cucco Lady Wake up sleeping Talon in Kakariko house using the Pocket Cucco; return to the Cucco Lady to receive the blue rooster Cojiro.
3. Odd Mushroom Grog (Lost Woods) Take Cojiro to the silent man (Grog) sitting under the tree in Lost Woods; he gives you the Odd Mushroom (3-minute timer!).
4. Odd Potion Granny's Potion Shop (Kakariko) Ride Epona to Kakariko; enter the secret potion shop behind the counter to deliver the mushroom for the Odd Potion.
5. Poacher's Saw Fado (Lost Woods) Return to the tree where Grog was sitting; give the potion to the Kokiri girl Fado to receive the Poacher's Saw.
6. Broken Goron's Sword Master Craftsman (Gerudo Valley) Cross the broken canyon bridge with Epona or the Longshot; trade the saw to the carpenter boss for the Broken Goron's Sword.
7. Prescription Biggoron (Death Mountain Summit) Climb to the summit of Death Mountain; Biggoron has eye irritation from volcanic ash and gives you the Prescription.
8. Eyeball Frog King Zora (Zora's Domain) Thaw King Zora with Blue Fire; show him the prescription to receive the perishable Eyeball Frog (3-minute timer!).
9. World's Finest Eye Drops Scientist (Lake Hylia Lab) Gallop across Hyrule Field on Epona to Lake Hylia; give the frog to the scientist to receive the Eye Drops (4-minute timer!).
10. Claim Check Biggoron (Death Mountain Summit) Ride Epona and warp or sprint past Death Mountain rolling boulders before the timer expires; deliver the eye drops for the Claim Check.
11. Biggoron's Sword Biggoron (Death Mountain Summit) Wait 3 days (play the Sun's Song 6 times), then present the Claim Check to claim your permanent Biggoron's Sword!

All 4 Empty Bottles Guide

Bottle #1 (Lon Lon Ranch): Win Talon's Super Cucco-catching minigame as Child Link.
Bottle #2 (Kakariko Village): Gather all 7 lost Cuccos scattered across Kakariko and throw them into the lady's pen.
Bottle #3 (Lake Hylia): Dive near the underwater grate leading to Zora's Domain to find the message in a bottle with Ruto's letter.
Bottle #4 (10 Big Poes in Hyrule Field): Hunt all 10 Big Poes while mounted on Epona and sell their souls to the Ghost Shop in Market ruins as Adult Link!
