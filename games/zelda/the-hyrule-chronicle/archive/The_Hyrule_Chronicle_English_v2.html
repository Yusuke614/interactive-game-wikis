Primeval Era: Null & The Golden Goddesses

天地創造以前 ・ 無の世界

Before time began, the primordial void Null swallowed all things. The three Golden Goddesses—Din (Power), Nayru (Wisdom), and Farore (Courage)—descended to cultivate the earth, establish immutable natural laws, and breathe life into all beings. Leaving behind the omnipotent golden relic, the Triforce, the goddesses departed, entrusting its stewardship to the divine protectress, the Goddess Hylia.

Key Chronicle Significance: Sealing of the primordial devourer Null and the genesis of Hyrule and the Triforce.
Setting: The formless Void of Nothingness and the dawn of Hyrule.
Key Artifacts: The Sacred Golden Relic — The Triforce.
Historical Outcome: Din, Nayru, and Farore cultivate order and life, sealing Null before departing to the heavens.

The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword

ゼルダの伝説 スカイウォードソード

The foundational legend where young knight apprentice Link journeys beneath the clouds to rescue childhood friend Zelda. Guiding the Goddess Sword with the spirit Fi, Link reforges the blade into the legendary Master Sword and overcomes Demise, who curses their bloodlines into an eternal cycle of battle.

Key Chronicle Significance: Forging of the Master Sword, reincarnation vow of Princess Zelda and Link, and Demise's eternal curse of wrath.
Setting: Skyloft and the ancient Surface (Sealed Grounds, Faron, Eldin, Lanayru).
Key Artifacts: Goddess Sword, Master Sword, Goddess Harp, Sailcloth, Triforce.
Historical Outcome: Zelda and Link choose to remain on the Surface, laying the spiritual groundwork for mortal civilization.

Founding of the Kingdom & The Temple of Time

ハイラル王国建国と時の神殿

To halt wars triggered by greedy sorcerers and mortal factions seeking the golden relic, the ancient Sage of Light, Rauru, constructed the Temple of Time over the resting place of the Master Sword, firmly sealing the Sacred Realm. The mortal descendants of Goddess Hylia formally founded the Kingdom of Hyrule.

Key Chronicle Significance: Sealing of the Sacred Realm doorway and the initial founding of the Royal Family of Hyrule.
Setting: Ruins of the Sealed Temple / Temple of Time, Hyrule Castle.
Key Artifacts: Door of Time, Master Sword, Spiritual Stones, Ocarina of Time.
Historical Outcome: The original Kingdom of Hyrule is established by Hylia's bloodline, distinct from the second founding in the Wild Era.
\n

The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap

ゼルダの伝説 ふしぎのぼうし

Tiny magical creatures known as the Minish (Picori) bestow the sacred Picori Blade and Light Force upon mankind. When the wicked wind mage Vaati shatters the blade and petrifies Princess Zelda, Link uses the magical hat Ezlo to shrink in size, reforging the blade into the Four Sword.

Key Chronicle Significance: Origin of the Four Sword and the containment of the wind sorcerer Vaati.
Setting: Hyrule Town, Minish Woods, Cloud Tops, Dark Hyrule Castle.
Key Artifacts: Picori Blade, Four Sword, Ezlo's Cap, Kinstones, Light Force.
Historical Outcome: Vaati is defeated and sealed; Princess Zelda uses Ezlo's magical cap to restore peace to Hyrule.

Creation & Unified
1998 N64 / 3DS

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time

ゼルダの伝説 時のオカリナ

The pivotal crux of the entire Zelda timeline. Gerudo leader Ganondorf exploits young Link's opening of the Door of Time to raid the Sacred Realm. Touching the Triforce with an unbalanced heart, the relic shatters: Ganondorf obtains Power, while Wisdom and Courage seek Zelda and Link. Awakening 7 years later as the Hero of Time, Link confronts destiny.

Key Chronicle Significance: Chronicle Pivot: The climactic confrontation splits history into three distinct timelines depending on the Hero's triumph or fall.
Setting: Kokiri Forest, Hyrule Field, Temple of Time, Sacred Realm, Ganon's Castle.
Key Artifacts: Ocarina of Time, Master Sword, Triforce (Power, Wisdom, Courage).
Historical Outcome: Reality irrevocably fractures into the Hero Defeated (Decline) branch, the Child Era, and the Adult Era.

Centuries after the Hero of Time falls and Ganon is sealed in the Dark World during the Imprisoning War, dark wizard Agahnim sacrifices descendants of the Sages to break the seal. A new hero awakens to rescue Princess Zelda, draws the Master Sword from the Lost Woods, conquers the Dark World, destroys Ganon, and recovers the complete Triforce.

The Tragedy of Princess Zelda I & Royal Decline

初代ゼルダの眠りと王国の衰退

Hyrule enters a Golden Age governed by benevolent monarchs using the Triforce. Fearing his prince's impure ambition, the King hides the Triforce of Courage. When the prince's sorcerer curses Princess Zelda into an unending magical slumber, the kingdom slowly fractures and falls into ruin.

Key Chronicle Significance: Explains why every royal maiden is named Zelda by royal decree, and the loss of the Triforce of Courage.
Setting: North Castle, ancient Hyrule royal courts.
Key Artifacts: Triforce of Courage, Magical scroll, King's Seal.
Historical Outcome: Princess Zelda I falls into magical stasis; the kingdom fractures as centuries of decay begin.

The Legend of Zelda

ゼルダの伝説 (THE HYRULE FANTASY)

The seminal adventure that launched the franchise. Demon King Ganon invades the declining kingdom, seizing the Triforce of Power. Princess Zelda shatters the Triforce of Wisdom into eight shards before her capture. Traveler Link unites the shards to slay Ganon inside Death Mountain.

Key Chronicle Significance: The original quest establishing the Triforce of Wisdom, Magical Sword, and the destruction of Ganon.
Setting: Wild and decayed Hyrule, Level 1–9 underworld dungeons, Spectacle Rock.
Key Artifacts: Wooden Sword, White Sword, Magical Sword, Silver Arrows, Triforce of Wisdom & Power.
Historical Outcome: Link defeats Ganon, frees Princess Zelda, and reunites the Triforces of Power and Wisdom.

Remote Antiquity Zonai Myth & Backstory

Descent of the Zonai & King Rauru's Founding

神話の時代／建国の時代 ・ 初代国王ラウルと封印戦

Bearer of heavenly Secret Stones, King Rauru—the first Zonai king—unites with Priestess Sonia of the surface to establish a revitalized Kingdom of Hyrule. When Gerudo chief Ganondorf murders Queen Sonia and swallows her Secret Stone to become the Demon King, Rauru and the Six Sages stage the Imprisoning War, sacrificing his own flesh to physically pin the Demon King's heart in an eternal slumber below Hyrule Castle.

Key Chronicle Significance: The official second founding of Hyrule, Princess Zelda's Draconification into the Light Dragon, and the thousand-year seal.
Setting: Ancient Great Plateau, Temple of Time, Forgotten Foundation beneath Hyrule Castle.
Key Artifacts: Secret Stones of Time & Light, Rauru's Right Arm, Ancient Master Sword.
Historical Outcome: Rauru seals Ganondorf; Zelda becomes the immortal Light Dragon to heal the Master Sword across millennia.

The Era of the Wild
2017 Wii U / Switch

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild

ゼルダの伝説 ブレス オブ ザ ワイルド

100 years after the devastating Great Calamity that obliterated Hyrule Castle and claimed the lives of the Four Champions, Link awakens from a century of regenerative slumber without memories. Traversing a ruined yet breathtaking open Hyrule, he liberates the corrupted Divine Beasts, reclaims the Master Sword, and vanquishes Calamity Ganon to rescue Princess Zelda.

Key Chronicle Significance: Open-Air Revolution: Redefined open-world game design while establishing an era where history across all past mythologies converges into ancient lore.
Setting: Vast post-apocalyptic Hyrule, Great Plateau, Divine Beasts Vah Ruta, Rudania, Medoh, Naboris.
Key Artifacts: Sheikah Slate, Master Sword (Restored), Champion abilities, Bow of Light.
Historical Outcome: Calamity Ganon is destroyed; Princess Zelda and Link begin rebuilding the Kingdom of Hyrule.
\n
The Era of the Wild
2023 Nintendo Switch

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom

ゼルダの伝説 ティアーズ オブ ザ キングダム

Investigating miasma deep beneath Hyrule Castle, Link and Zelda discover the mummified corpse of the ancient Demon King Ganondorf. Unbound, Ganondorf lifts the castle skyward in the Upheaval, shattering the Master Sword. Hurled into the distant founding past, Zelda undergoes Draconification into the Light Dragon to bathe the Master Sword in sacred power for tens of thousands of years. Wielding Rauru's arm, Link conquers Sky, Surface, and Depths to destroy the Demon King.

Key Chronicle Significance: The Grand Narrative Loop: Connects the ancient Zonai founding with the ultimate redemption of Princess Zelda and restoration of the Master Sword.
Setting: Hyrule across three vertical planes: Sky Islands, Surface, and subterranean Depths.
Key Artifacts: Rauru's Right Arm (Ultrahand, Fuse, Ascend, Recall), Secret Stones, Dragon-Tempered Master Sword.
Historical Outcome: Demon Dragon Ganondorf is eradicated; ancestral light restores Princess Zelda to mortal form.
