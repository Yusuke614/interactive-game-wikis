1. Basics, Switch 2 Controls & Ocarina of Time Remake Features
Nintendo Switch 2 Remake

Grounded directly on the official IGN Ocarina of Time Remake Wiki, this section covers all core gameplay overhauls, Nintendo Switch 2 controls, the Threads of Light sidequest tracker, Fairy Writing healing hints, and essential opening milestones in Hyrule:

🌟 Core Ocarina of Time Remake Overhauls & World Additions
Major Gameplay & Dungeon Enhancements
Expanded Wallets (200 / 500 / 1,000R) & Economy Economy

Link's starting wallet holds 200 Rupees right from the beginning of the game, the Adult's Wallet (10 Gold Skulltulas) holds 500 Rupees, and the Giant's Wallet (30 Gold Skulltulas) holds 1,000 Rupees (IGN Wallet Guide). In Kokiri Forest, generous Blue Rupees are hidden behind Link's House, behind Mido's house, and on the shop awning (IGN Kokiri Guide).

Full 3D Free Camera in Castle Town Exploration

Hyrule Castle Town features complete 360-degree Right-Stick 3D camera control with seamless transitions from the drawbridge through Market Square to the Temple of Time, alongside new market townsfolk and interactive stalls (IGN 29 Remake Changes).

Streamlined Dungeons & Instant Boot Toggle Dungeons

The Water Temple features illuminated route pipes, consolidated small-key paths, and instant D-Pad Up / Down toggling for the Iron Boots and Hover Boots without opening the pause menu. The Forest Temple also adds a vertical elevator shortcut after the block-pushing room (IGN Remake Changes).

Overhauled Combat & Enemy Telegraphing Combat

Skulltulas spin around to expose their vulnerable underbelly when struck with your sword (IGN Deku Tree Guide), Floormasters telegraph ceiling drops with color-changing spotlights, King Dodongo summons Baby Dodongos when slamming into walls, and Dark Link actively parries and breaks crouch-stab loops.

Full English Voice Acting & Deeper Dialogue Presentation

Main story cutscenes feature full English voice acting—starring Erica Mendez (Princess Zelda & Sheik), Matt Mercer (Ganondorf), and Lisa Reimold (Navi)—paired with expanded character dialogue and cinematic spell animations (IGN Remake Overview).

5th Empty Bottle & 5-Big-Poe Ghost Shop Rule Inventory

Link can collect 5 Empty Bottles total: Bottle #1 is earned right in the Castle Town Potion Shop by catching an escaped bug (granting a free Red Potion + bottle), and Bottle #5 at the Ghost Shop only requires turning in 5 out of 10 Big Poes (500 points) (IGN Empty Bottles Guide)!

Business Scrub Seeds & Persistent Princess Ruto Quality of Life

Chests in Dodongo's Cavern and Death Mountain Crater reward Business Scrub Seeds, which can be thrown down almost anywhere to summon an emergency merchant! Additionally, in Lord Jabu-Jabu's Belly, Princess Ruto stays wherever you leave her sitting even if you exit the dungeon.

HUD Skulltula Gem & Great Fairy Offerings Exploration

The 20-Skulltula Stone of Agony adds a flashing HUD Gem Indicator that pulses faster as you near any hidden Gold Skulltula (IGN Dodongo Guide). Great Fairy Fountains are tracked via the "To the Great Fairy" Thread of Light and ask for escalating Rupee offerings (starting at 25R on Death Mountain Summit for Din's Fire + Magic Meter).

🎮 Switch 2 Controls, Threads of Light & Fairy Writing Mechanics
Modern Interface & Quest Systems
Switch 2 Button Mapping & R/L Quick Wheel Controls

Y swings your sword (hold Y for Spin Attack), B puts your weapon away (sheathes weapon), X jumps, A (Tap) rolls, and A (Hold) dashes. Hold R or L to open the radial quick-equip item wheel, press ZR to use your equipped ranged/action item, and use D-Pad Up/Down for Boots/Masks, D-Pad Left for Ocarina, and D-Pad Right for Navi (IGN Controls Guide).

ZL Snap-Targeting & Gyroscope Precision Aiming Aiming

Hold ZL to lock onto enemies or switches and press ZR to fire, or enter free-aim mode with the Fairy Slingshot, Fairy Bow, Boomerang, and Hookshot/Longshot using the Right Stick combined with smooth motion gyroscope aiming (IGN Deku Tree Guide).

Threads of Light / Threads of Time Quest Log Quest Tracker

Sidequests and multi-part objectives are tracked in your menu via Threads of Light—including "A Song of the Royal Family" (Sun's Song), "To the Great Fairy" (All 6 Great Fairies), and "From a Pocket Egg Comes..." (Biggoron Sword).

Fairy Writing & Musical Healing Secrets Fairy Thoughts

Look for twinkling blue Fairy Writing symbols on walls and high dungeon ledges (such as inside the Royal Family's Tomb and on Floor 5F of the Fire Temple). Have Navi inspect the riddle and play the hinted Ocarina melody (like the Sun's Song) to summon a Fairy essence that fully heals your hearts!

Essential Beginner Milestones

Find the Kokiri Sword: Climb the vine wall behind Link's House to the Kokiri Training Center, crawl through the narrow tunnel, dodge the rolling boulder, and open the chest for the Kokiri Sword (IGN Kokiri Walkthrough).
Buy the Deku Shield (40 Rupees): With your starting 200-Rupee Wallet, collect the Blue Rupees behind Link's House, behind Mido's house, and atop the shop awning, then buy the Deku Shield at the Kokiri Shop (IGN Kokiri Walkthrough).
Claim Your First 2 Empty Bottles Early: Visit the Castle Town Potion Shop and catch the escaped bug for the shopkeeper to earn Bottle #1 pre-filled with a free Red Potion, then win Talon's Super Cucco minigame at Lon Lon Ranch for Bottle #2 (IGN All Empty Bottle Locations).
Acquire the Free Hylian Shield in Kakariko Graveyard: In the row of graves just before the steps up to the Royal Tomb, pull back the second grave on the right, stun the ReDead inside with the Sun's Song, and open the chest for a free Hylian Shield (IGN Kakariko Walkthrough)!

4. Remake How-To Guides, Threads of Light & Rupee Farming
Threads of Light • Spells, Arrows & Rupees

Grounded on the official IGN Ocarina of Time Remake How-To Guides, this section covers key Threads of Light sidequests, Fairy Writing riddles, elemental arrow trials, secret grottos, and the fastest Rupee farming routes in Hyrule:

How to Get The Sun's Song Thread of Light

Interact with the two harp-shaped graves flanking the Royal Crest at the back of Kakariko Graveyard to battle the Composer Brothers (Sharp the Elder and Flat the Younger—wait for them to materialize before striking). Play Zelda's Lullaby on the Royal Crest to open the tomb, sprint past the ReDeads to learn the Sun's Song, and play it at the twinkling blue Fairy Writing pillar for a full heal (IGN Sun's Song Guide)!

How to Farm Rupees Easily Max 1,000R Wallet

To fill your 500R Adult's Wallet or 1,000R Giant's Wallet quickly: (1) Smash the Guardhouse pots in Castle Town; (2) Defeat escalating waves of Stalchildren in Hyrule Field at night so giant ones spawn and drop Blue/Red Rupee clusters; (3) Catch fish in Zora's Domain in Empty Bottles and sell them in Castle Town; or (4) Defeat the 3 Skull Kids in the Lost Woods as Adult Link for a 200-Rupee Golden Rupee each (IGN Rupee Farming Guide)!

What Are Fairy Thoughts / Fairy Writing Healing Riddles

Throughout Hyrule's tombs, caverns, and temples (including the Royal Family's Tomb and the 5F south ledge of the Fire Temple), you'll spot twinkling blue Fairy Writing symbols. Press D-Pad Right when Navi targets them to read the fairy's musical riddle, then play the matching Ocarina song to release a healing fairy essence!

To the Great Fairy (All 6 Offerings) Thread of Light

Tracked via the "To the Great Fairy" Thread of Light (IGN Great Fairy Guide), each Great Fairy Fountain asks for a Rupee offering after playing Zelda's Lullaby: 25R on Death Mountain Summit (Din's Fire + Magic), 50R at Hyrule Castle (Gift of Improved Spin), 100R at Zora's Fountain (Farore's Wind), and 200R in Death Mountain Crater (Gift of Doubled Magic).

How to Get Fire, Ice & Light Arrows Elemental Bows

• Fire Arrows: After clearing the Water Temple, stand on the stone slab above the Lake Hylia warp pad at dawn and shoot an arrow into the rising sun.
• Ice Arrows: Collect at least 7 Small Keys inside the Gerudo Training Ground trials to unlock the central treasure vault.
• Light Arrows: Bestowed by Princess Zelda inside the Temple of Time after awakening all 6 Sages.

How to Get Iron Boots & Blue Fire Ice Cavern

Hop across the ice floes in Zora's Fountain as Adult Link to enter the Ice Cavern. Bottle Blue Fire from the pedestals (or buy it for 300R in Kakariko Potion Shop) to melt red ice barriers, defeat the White Wolfos in the final chamber to claim the Iron Boots (equipped via D-Pad Up/Down), and learn the Serenade of Water from Sheik.

How to Unlock Epona & Link's House Cow Lon Lon Ranch

Learn Epona's Song from Malon as Child Link. As Adult Link, pay Ingo 10 Rupees twice at Lon Lon Ranch, play Epona's Song to mount Epona, beat Ingo in two back-to-back races, and leap the outer ranch fence! Afterwards, beat Malon's obstacle course race in under 40 seconds to have a free milk-giving Cow delivered to Link's House in Kokiri Forest (IGN Grove Guide)!

Happy Mask Shop & Mask of Truth D-Pad Masks

Borrow and sell 4 masks from the Castle Town Happy Mask Shop as Child Link: Keaton Mask (Kakariko Death Mountain Guard), Skull Mask (Skull Kid in Lost Woods), Spooky Mask (Graveyard Kid during day), and Bunny Hood (Running Man in Hyrule Field after Jabu-Jabu). This unlocks the Mask of Truth to hear NPC thoughts and Sheikah Stone lore (IGN Jabu-Jabu Guide)!

In The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake, Link starts with the Fairy Ocarina and upgrades to the royal Ocarina of Time. Below are the complete Nintendo Switch 2 button and microphone controls alongside single-row melody sequences for all 12 sacred songs and the Scarecrow's Song:

🎵 Nintendo Switch 2 Remake Ocarina Control Scheme & Mic Audio Input

The 2026 Nintendo Switch 2 Remake thoroughly overhauls and modernizes the Ocarina experience with dedicated button shortcuts, an instant on-screen songbook overlay, pitch bending, and optional real-time microphone audio input:

🎮 Button Mapping & In-Game Songbook
  • Draw / Play Ocarina: Press D-Pad Left (D-Pad Right is reserved for Navi).
  • Quick-Access Songbook: Press ZL while holding the Ocarina to pull up your learned songs and note sequences on-screen without pausing!
  • 5-Pitch Layout:
    L Low D  |  R F  |  A A  |  Y B  |  X High D
  • Ascending 5-Pitch Layout: From lowest to highest pitch, the 5 notes are L → R → A → Y → X.
🎼 Pitch Bends, Vibrato & Dynamics
  • Pitch Bends (Sharp / Flat): Tilt the Left Control Stick Up to raise pitch by a half-step (♯), or tilt Down to lower pitch by a half-step (♭).
  • Vibrato / Modulation: Rapidly wiggle the Left Control Stick Left & Right while holding a note to introduce expressive acoustic vibrato.
  • Volume & Tone Dynamics: Modulate acoustic note volume and pressure with ZR to play soft or loud dynamics.
🎙️ Microphone Audio Input (Humming, Whistling & Real Instruments)

Toggle "Microphone Input" to ON in the game's Options menu. An "Inputting audio..." HUD prompt appears whenever the Ocarina is drawn, letting the Switch 2's built-in console microphone analyze musical pitches in real time:

• Humming & Whistling: Hum, whistle, or sing the 5 solfège pitches (D, F, A, B, High D) directly toward the microphone.
• Real Physical Instruments: Play melodies on acoustic guitars, keyboards, recorders, flutes, or the official Nintendo Store physical Ocarina of Time!
• Minus (-) Button: Practice audio input and test humming pitch calibration before performing.
• Plus (+) Button: Reset audio input to immediately clear any mis-detected pitch notes.
• Hybrid Play: Seamlessly hum opening notes and finish the song using physical buttons!
