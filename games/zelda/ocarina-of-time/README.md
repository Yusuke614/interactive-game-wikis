# The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake — Complete Interactive Guidebook

An interactive, single-page completion guide and mechanics reference for **The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake** on **Nintendo Switch 2**. Grounded directly on the official [IGN Ocarina of Time Remake Wiki](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake), styled with the iconic forest and Triforce aesthetic of Hyrule.

---

## 🗡️ Overview & Quick Specs

* **Game**: *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake* (Nintendo Switch 2)
* **Current Version**: `v13` (`The Legend of Zelda Ocarina of Time Remake Wiki_v13.html`)
* **Trackable Tasks**: 159 interactive checkable items (including all 10 dungeons, all 39 equipment/bag/Great Fairy upgrades in Section 8, all 5 Empty Bottles in Section 9, all 36 Heart Pieces, all 100 Gold Skulltulas, all 12 Ocarina songs, and the 11-step Biggoron Sword trading sequence)
* **Cross-Section Navigation**: Standardized Previous and Next section navigation buttons across all 10 section pages with responsive mobile stacking (`@media (max-width: 600px)`) and loop-back to Section 1
* **Authoritative Source**: [IGN Ocarina of Time Remake Wiki](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake)
* **Hosting Format**: Pure HTML5 / CSS3 / Vanilla JS single-page application (SPA)

## 📜 10-Section Guide Architecture (IGN Remake Sequence)

1. **Basics, Switch 2 Controls & Ocarina of Time Remake Features (`sec_basics`)**: Covers core Remake overhauls (200/500/1,000-Rupee wallets, full 3D free camera in Castle Town, streamlined Water & Forest Temples, instant `D-Pad Up/Down` Boots/Masks toggle, physical combat telegraphing, full English voice acting, 5th Empty Bottle & 5-Big-Poe Ghost Shop rule, Business Scrub Seeds, persistent Princess Ruto, and the Stone of Agony HUD Gem radar) alongside Switch 2 button mappings, `R/L` quick wheel, `ZL/ZR` & gyro aiming, **Threads of Light** quest tracking, **Fairy Writing** healing riddles, and essential beginner milestones.
2. **Main Quest Walkthrough — Child Era (`sec_walkthrough_child`)**: [Kokiri Forest](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/Kokiri_Forest_Walkthrough), [Inside the Great Deku Tree](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/Great_Deku_Tree_Dungeon_Walkthrough), [Hyrule Castle, Kakariko & Lon Lon Ranch](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/Spiritual_Stone_of_Fire_-_Kakariko_Village_Walkthrough), [Dodongo's Cavern & Death Mountain](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/Dodongo_Cavern_Dungeon_Walkthrough), [Inside Lord Jabu-Jabu's Belly](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/Lord_Jabu-Jabu%27s_Belly_Dungeon_Walkthrough), and the Temple of Time Master Sword awakening.
3. **Main Quest Walkthrough — Adult Era (`sec_walkthrough_adult`)**: [Kakariko Graveyard & Sacred Grove](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/Forest_Temple_Mysteries_-_Graveyard_and_Sacred_Grove_Walkthrough), [Forest Temple](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/Forest_Temple_Dungeon_Walkthrough), [Fire Temple](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/Fire_Temple_Dungeon_Walkthrough), Ice Cavern & Water Temple, Bottom of the Well & Shadow Temple, Gerudo Fortress & Spirit Temple, and Ganon's Castle.
4. **Remake How-To Guides, Threads of Light & Rupee Farming (`sec_master_quest`)**: Dedicated Switch 2 Remake guides grounded on [IGN's How-To Guides](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/How-To_Guides_-_The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Ocarina_of_Time_Remake), including [How to Get The Sun's Song ("A Song of the Royal Family")](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/How_to_Get_The_Sun%27s_Song_-_A_Song_of_the_Royal_Family_Sidequest), [How to Farm Rupees Easily](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/How_to_Farm_Rupees_Easily), Fairy Writing / Fairy Thoughts explained, [To the Great Fairy](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/To_the_Great_Fairy_-_All_Great_Fairy_Fountain_Locations), Elemental Arrows, Iron Boots & Blue Fire, Epona & the 40-second Cow obstacle course, and the Happy Mask Shop (`Mask of Truth`).
5. **All 36 Pieces of Heart Guide (`sec_heart_pieces`)**: Complete checklist of all 36 Pieces of Heart across Hyrule ([IGN Heart Piece Guide](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/All_Heart_Piece_Locations)).
6. **All 100 Gold Skulltulas Guide (`sec_gold_skulltulas`)**: All 100 Gold Skulltula tokens and House of Skulltula Remake rewards (500R Adult's Wallet, HUD Radar Stone of Agony, 1,000R Giant's Wallet, Bombchus, Piece of Heart, and infinite 200 Rupees) ([IGN Gold Skulltula Guide](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/All_Gold_Skulltula_Locations)).
7. **Ocarina Songs & Melodies (`sec_ocarina_songs`)**: Complete Nintendo Switch 2 Ocarina control scheme (`D-Pad Left` equip, `ZL` Songbook, `L/R/A/Y/X` 5-pitch buttons, analog pitch bend/vibrato, `ZR` acoustic resonance, and console Microphone audio mode with `-` Practice and `+` Reset) plus single-row button notations for all 12 sacred melodies and the Scarecrow's Song ([IGN Song Guide](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/All_Song_Locations)).
8. **Equipment, Capacity Upgrades & Great Fairy Fountains (`sec_equipment`)**: 39 individually checkable items across Swords, Shields, Tunics & Boots, Gauntlets/Scales/Lens, [Item Bag Expansions](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/All_Deku-Stick_Bag_Upgrade_Locations) (Sticks, Seeds, Nuts), [Wallets](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/All_Wallet_Upgrade_Locations)/Bomb Bags/Quivers/Arrows/Business Scrub Seeds, and all 6 [Great Fairy Fountains](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/To_the_Great_Fairy_-_All_Great_Fairy_Fountain_Locations) (*Din's Fire*, *Gift of Improved Spin*, *Farore's Wind*, [*Gift of Doubled Magic*](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/How_to_Get_Gift_of_Doubled_Magic), *Nayru's Love*, and *Gift of Halved Damage*).
9. **Side Quests, Biggoron Sword & Empty Bottles (`sec_side_quests`)**: The 11-step [Biggoron Sword Trading Quest ("From a Pocket Egg Comes...")](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/How_to_Get_the_Biggoron_Sword_-_Trading_Sidequest) with Remake item names and timers, plus the checkable table for [All 5 Empty Bottles](https://www.ign.com/wikis/ocarina-of-time-remake/All_Empty_Bottle_Locations).
10. **Dungeon Bosses & Mini-Bosses Strategy Guide (`sec_bosses`)**: Complete weak-point phases and battle tactics for all 10 major bosses and Dark Link.
