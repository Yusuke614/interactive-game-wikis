Things to Do First in AC Black Flag Resynced
Beginner's Roadmap

Setting sail across the vast Caribbean can be overwhelming. Follow these initial milestones to establish early naval dominance and character efficiency:

1. Synchronize All 9 Viewpoints in Havana: Unlocks crucial fast-travel nodes and maps every tavern, shop, collectible, and shanty in the capital.
2. Acquire Hidden Blades & Your First Flintlock: Obtained early in Sequence 2 during the Governor Torres and Stede Bonnet missions.
3. Plunder Upgrade Materials for the Jackdaw: Focus on boarding lower-level Schooners and Brigs in the northern easy zones to hoard Metal, Wood, and Cloth.
4. Upgrade the Great Inagua Hideout Harbourmaster: Unlocked during Sequence 4. Upgrading the harbourmaster unlocks higher-tier Jackdaw upgrades and local repairs.
5. Craft More Pistol Holsters Immediately: Hunt ocelots on Abaco Island and wild boars to craft Holster II and III, allowing Edward to chain four consecutive pistol shots without reloading.
6. Complete Early Officer Quests: Talk to Adéwalé at the Great Inagua Tavern to recruit Lucy Baldwin for essential ship defense.
7. Capture Early Forts (Punta Guarico): Eliminates maritime restricted zones, unmasks surrounding sea charts, and unlocks naval contracts and fast travel.
8. Prioritize Core Ship Upgrades: Invest your first Reales and metals into Armored Hull, Mortar, and Broadside Cannons.

What's New in Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced
Remake Innovations

Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced reimagines the 2013 pirate masterpiece with modern visual fidelity, refined responsive parkour and combat mechanics, and major structural narrative enhancements designed for modern players.

Rifts & Animus AI EGO Story Overhaul

The traditional real-world office walking segments at Abstergo Entertainment have been replaced by surreal, dimension-bending Rifts. Within these Rifts, an enigmatic Animus AI construct named EGO (Emotion Agent) manipulates genetic timelines, tests Edward Kenway's resolve, and creates philosophical anomalies.

Recruitable Officers System Crew Mechanics

Following the restoration of the Great Inagua Hideout, Adéwalé introduces unique specialist officers that can be recruited to the Jackdaw. Officers like Lucy Baldwin (grants enhanced damage reduction braces during naval volleys), Deadman (explosive cannon artillery), and Padre (crew survivability and boarding bonuses) provide specialized passive and active combat perks.

Dark Animus & Anomalies World Events

Accessible directly from the Jackdaw's captain's cabin terminal, the Dark Animus tracks dynamic temporal anomalies across the Caribbean archipelago. These include limited-time combat encounters, such as hunting down corrupted Captain Guards in pirate havens and Spanish fortifications.

EGO Boss Encounters Combat Evolution

Unlike the original parry-and-kill combat, Resynced features complex multi-phase boss engagements. The climactic encounter against EGO in the True Purpose Rift incorporates telegraphed unblockables, AoE shockwaves, floating barrier shields, and laser sweeps requiring timed dodges and ammo management.
